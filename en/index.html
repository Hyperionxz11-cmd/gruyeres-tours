The Gruyère region

Between stone, pastures and peaks.

In the heart of the canton of Fribourg, the Gruyère region brings together a medieval town, alpine pastures where Gruyère AOP is born, a lake and the first ridges of the Prealps. This is the landscape we want to share with you, simply and at your own pace.
